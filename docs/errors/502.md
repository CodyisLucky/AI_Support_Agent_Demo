# Error 502

## What the shopper sees

"The network is unavailable. Nothing was changed. Try again in a few minutes."

## When it happens

The shop could not reach the network for this action. Two demo triggers do this:

- Card `4000000000000502` at checkout. They stay on checkout. The order is not placed.
- Username `networkdown` on sign-in, create-account, or checkout while creating an account. Sign-in does not check the password, because the account store could not be reached. The account is not created.

This is the general network, not the payment processor. Processor-only failure is 501.

## Likely cause

A connection the shop needs was down, or one of those demo triggers was used.

## What to try

Try again in a few minutes. Signing in as `networkdown` will fail the same way every time. Creating that username will also fail. Use a normal username and, at checkout, a different card number. Nothing was charged and no account was saved for this attempt.

## Log line

`code` is `502`. `user_id` is `null` when the account store could not be reached. For the card trigger while already signed in, `user_id` is that account. `detail` is `username networkdown` or `last4 0502`.
