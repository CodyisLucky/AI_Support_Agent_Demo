# Error 508

## What the shopper sees

The Help chat answers with a short note from the shop, not from the assistant. It says the assistant hit a technical problem, that nothing in their account or orders was changed, and that they can email the team with their conversation ID. The assistant is paused for the rest of that chat.

## When it happens

The support assistant could not produce a trustworthy answer because something failed around it:

- the AI provider could not be reached, timed out, rate limited the shop, or rejected the API key;
- no API key is set, or the provider chosen in `agent.env` is not connected;
- the provider declined to answer;
- the assistant used up its lookup rounds without an answer;
- the shopper's orders or error records could not be read.

A handoff the assistant chooses itself, such as a question the docs do not cover or a refund request, is not an error and is not logged as 508. Usage limits and off-topic pauses are 509.

## Likely cause

A provider outage, a missing or wrong key in `.env`, the local model not running, or a storage fault. `detail` says which.

## What to try

Email the team with the conversation ID. Closing the browser and coming back starts a new chat, and the assistant answers again once the cause is fixed. Nothing was changed by the failed reply.

## Log line

`code` is `508`. `user_id` is the signed-in account, or `null` for a guest. `detail` is the failure, such as `missing ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` or `provider not connected: openai`. It does not include what the shopper typed.
