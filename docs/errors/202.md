# Error 202

## What the shopper sees

The item name, then "is out of stock." From the cart, the banner also says to remove it and try again. The shop card for that item says "Out of stock" and the add button is disabled.

## When it happens

Warm Highlighter (`highlighter`) has no stock. Posting it to the cart writes this code. Checkout writes it again if that item is still in the cart, and the order is not placed.

Other products and both services are in stock.

## Likely cause

They tried to buy Warm Highlighter. Stock is part of the shop listing, not a payment failure.

## What to try

Remove Warm Highlighter from the cart and check out with the other items. There is no waitlist and no substitute shade. Cream Eyeshadow and Petal Cream Blush are different products.

## Log line

`code` is `202`. `user_id` is the signed-in account, or `null` for a guest. `detail` is the item id, or several ids separated by commas.
