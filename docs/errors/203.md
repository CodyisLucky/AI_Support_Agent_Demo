# Error 203

## What the shopper sees

"Your cart is empty."

## When it happens

They opened checkout, or submitted it, with nothing in the cart. The shop sends them back to the cart.

## Likely cause

The cart was already empty, or the order just placed cleared it and they went to checkout again.

## What to try

Add an in-stock item from the shop, then check out. This is not a payment or sign-in failure.

## Log line

`code` is `203`. `user_id` is the signed-in account, or `null` for a guest. `detail` is empty.
