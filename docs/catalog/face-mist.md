# Dew Face Mist

## What it is

A light leave-on spray to refresh bare skin or makeup. It is a hydrating mist, not a setting spray and not a toner. Stay Setting Spray is the product that helps makeup last. Calming Toner is the after-cleansing step.

## Price and size

$18.00 for 100 ml. Two sprays, once or twice a day, lasts about a month. Soaking the face uses it up and can melt makeup.

## Ingredients

Water, glycerin, panthenol, beta-glucan, and a mild preservative.

Not in this product: alcohol, fragrance, essential oils, SPF, or the polymers that Stay Setting Spray uses to hold makeup. It adds a little water and glycerin. It does not lock a look in place.

## Benefits

- A quick refresh when skin feels tight under makeup or in dry indoor air.
- Can dampen the face before Dew Hydrating Serum if the shopper skipped toner.
- Fragrance-free and alcohol-free, so it is less likely to sting than a scented facial spray.
- Fine over Veil Skin Tint if sprayed from far enough away in a light pass.

## Drawbacks

- It will not set makeup. Cream blush, concealer, and tint can move or look patchy if the mist is sprayed too close or too wet.
- It is not sunscreen and it does not refresh SPF. Sheer Mineral SPF still has to be reapplied the real way.
- Glycerin in a humid room can feel sticky if the shopper oversprays.
- It is a weak moisturizer. Dry skin still needs serum and cream.

## Works best when

Held about 20 cm (8 inches) from the face, two sprays, either before serum on bare skin or as a light pass over finished makeup when the face feels dry. Let it dry before adding Soft Setting Powder. Powder over a wet mist clumps.

## Less effective when

The shopper wants makeup to last through a long day or a workout. That is Stay Setting Spray, used after the makeup is finished. Also ineffective as the only hydration step.

## How to use

1. Shake once.
2. Close the mouth. Eyes can stay open if the shopper does not aim at them. The formula should not sting. If it does, stop.
3. Hold the bottle about 20 cm away. Two sprays in an X or a single pass.
4. Press with clean hands only if the face is bare. Over makeup, let it dry. Do not pat hard.
5. Bare-skin order: mist, then Dew Hydrating Serum, then moisturizer.
6. Makeup order: finish the face, including Stay Setting Spray if using it. Use this mist only later, and lightly, when the face feels dry.

## Pairs with

- Before Dew Hydrating Serum on bare skin.
- Not a replacement for Calming Toner, Stay Setting Spray, or Sheer Mineral SPF.
- If makeup looks blotchy after misting, the bottle was too close. Next time, more distance and fewer sprays.

## Shopper questions

### Is this a setting spray?

No. Stay Setting Spray is the setting product. This mist is water, glycerin, panthenol, and beta-glucan. It refreshes. It does not hold makeup.

### Can I spray it over sunscreen to reapply SPF?

No. It has no SPF. Reapply Sheer Mineral SPF.

### Will it ruin my makeup?

A far, light spray usually will not. A close, wet spray can lift Veil Skin Tint and Soft Concealer. If the look is cream-heavy, skip the mist or use it only before makeup.

### Can I use it as a toner?

You can dampen skin with it before serum, but Calming Toner is the product made for that step and has centella and allantoin. The mist is simpler.

### Is there alcohol or fragrance?

No alcohol and no fragrance.
