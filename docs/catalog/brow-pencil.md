# Brow Pencil

## What it is

A slim, waxy brow pencil in one shade, Soft Brown, with a spoolie on the other end. It is for hair-like strokes in sparse brows. It is not a pomade and not a tinted gel. Lumen does not sell those.

## Price and size

$16.00 for a 0.09 g pencil. Kept sharp and used in strokes, it lasts a few months. Scribbling a block of color wears it down fast.

## Ingredients

Hydrogenated vegetable oil, waxes, iron oxides for Soft Brown, and tocopherol. The pencil is self-sharpening only in the sense that the shopper must sharpen it. A sharpener is not included.

Not in this product: fiber gels, growth serums, or a second color. Soft Brown is a medium neutral brown. It is too dark for pale blonde brows and too light for black brows.

## Benefits

- A fine tip can imitate hairs instead of drawing a solid bar.
- Wax holds the stroke in place better than a powder on dry skin.
- The spoolie blends harsh lines. Use it after every few strokes.
- Easy daytime brow for shoppers who do not want a bold filled shape.

## Drawbacks

- One shade. Cool pale blondes and very dark brows will not get a match.
- A dull tip makes thick, obvious lines. Sharpen it.
- It will not grow brows or tint the skin the way a henna or a dye does.
- On very oily brows it can fade by evening. Stay Setting Spray helps a little. It is not budge-proof.
- The spoolie from this pencil should not be dipped into Lift Mascara.

## Works best when

Brows are medium brown and a bit sparse. Short strokes in the direction of hair, then a spoolie pass. A sharp tip is required.

## Less effective when

The shopper wants a solid Instagram block brow, a blonde pencil, or a black pencil. Also less effective if the tip is blunt or the strokes go against the hair direction.

## How to use

1. Brush brows up with the spoolie.
2. Sharpen the pencil. A sharp tip is the whole technique.
3. Light strokes where hairs are missing, mostly the tail and the front edge. Do not outline a harsh box.
4. Spoolie again to soften.
5. Stop when the brow looks filled in person, not in a magnified mirror only.
6. Makeup spray, if used, comes after the whole face from about 20 cm away.

## Pairs with

- Any Lumen face routine. It does not depend on Veil Skin Tint.
- Stay Setting Spray at the end if the brows fade.
- Do not use the spoolie as a mascara comb unless it is washed and dried first.

## Shopper questions

### What color is Soft Brown?

A medium neutral brown. It is not ash blonde, not auburn, and not black. If the shopper's brows are much lighter or darker, say so. There is no other pencil.

### How do I get a natural look?

Sharp tip, short strokes in the hair direction, then spoolie. Do not color the whole brow in like a marker.

### Will this help my brows grow?

No. It is makeup. It does not contain a growth serum.

### My pencil looks waxy and thick.

The tip is blunt. Sharpen it. A dull wax pencil always looks heavy.

### Can I fill the whole brow?

You can, but the product is designed for sparse spots. A full fill in one medium brown can look like a sticker if the skin shows through as a solid shape. Strokes look more like hair.
