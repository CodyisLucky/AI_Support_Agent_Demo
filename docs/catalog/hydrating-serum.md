# Dew Hydrating Serum

## What it is

A water-light leave-on serum for skin that feels tight or looks dull from dryness. It is a humectant serum, not a treatment for wrinkles, acne, or dark spots. The shop summary mentions glycerin. That is the main ingredient, backed by two weights of hyaluronate.

## Price and size

$42.00 for 30 ml. Two to three drops is a use. A bottle lasts about two months at twice a day.

## Ingredients

Water, glycerin, sodium hyaluronate, hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid, panthenol, beta-glucan, and a mild preservative.

Not in this product: fragrance, essential oils, alcohol, niacinamide, retinol, vitamin C, or exfoliating acids. Do not tell a shopper it is a brightening or anti-aging serum.

## Benefits

- Light layers of water-binding ingredients without a heavy film.
- Sits well under Soft Daily Moisturizer and makeup when used in a small amount.
- Useful morning and night.
- A simple formula for shoppers who react to fragrance.

## Drawbacks

- In a dry room or a dry climate it can feel tight again if it is not sealed with a moisturizer. Hyaluronate draws water. If the air is very dry and nothing occlusive follows, skin can feel drier.
- It will not replace Soft Daily Moisturizer or Rest Night Cream.
- It does not exfoliate, so flaky skin from a damaged barrier may still flake until the shopper eases off scrubs and clay masks.
- At $42.00 it is one of the more expensive leave-ons. The value is the simple hydrating layer, not a medical result.

## Works best when

Skin feels tight after cleansing, makeup looks crepey from dehydration, or a shopper wants a thin layer before moisturizer. Apply to damp skin.

## Less effective when

The shopper wants acne control, pigment fading, or wrinkle treatment. Also a weak standalone product for very dry skin in winter. Those shoppers still need Rest Night Cream or Glow Face Oil on top.

## How to use

1. Cleanse. Leave the face slightly damp, or press on Calming Toner.
2. Use 2 or 3 drops for the whole face. More can pill under sunscreen.
3. Press, do not rub hard. Neck optional.
4. Within one minute, apply Soft Daily Moisturizer in the morning or Rest Night Cream at night.
5. Morning continues with Sheer Mineral SPF, then makeup if wanted.

## Pairs with

- Before it: Cloud Cream Cleanser or Fresh Gel Cleanser, then Calming Toner.
- After it: Soft Daily Moisturizer, Rest Night Cream, Sheer Mineral SPF.
- Skip layering it with a large amount of Glow Face Oil before sunscreen. That combination can make Sheer Mineral SPF pill.

## Shopper questions

### Is this a hyaluronic acid serum?

Yes. It uses sodium hyaluronate and a smaller hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid, plus glycerin. It is not pure hyaluronic acid in water and nothing else.

### Can I use it alone?

You can, but Lumen does not recommend it as the only leave-on step, especially in dry air. Follow with a moisturizer.

### Will it pill under makeup?

A heavy hand pills under Sheer Mineral SPF and Veil Skin Tint. Two or three drops, pressed in, then moisturizer, is the amount that behaves.

### Can oily skin use it?

Yes, if the skin is dehydrated but shiny. Use 2 drops and Soft Daily Moisturizer, not Rest Night Cream.

### How is this different from Dew Face Mist?

The serum is a measured leave-on layer with more humectant. The mist is a light spray to refresh. The mist does not replace the serum.
