# Cloud Cream Cleanser

## What it is

A rinse-off cream cleanser for dry or easily irritated skin. It melts sunscreen and light makeup without a tight, foamy finish. It is the gentler of the two Lumen cleansers. Fresh Gel Cleanser is the one for oily skin.

## Price and size

$24.00 for 150 ml. One bottle is about two to three months of once-a-day use, or six weeks if used morning and night.

## Ingredients

Water, glycerin, coco-glucoside, squalane, ceramide NP, colloidal oat, panthenol, and a mild preservative. pH about 5.5.

Not in this product: fragrance, essential oils, alcohol, sulfates, acids, or scrubby beads.

## Benefits

- Cleans without the squeaky feel that makes dry skin feel worse.
- Ceramide NP, glycerin, and oat support a comfortable rinse.
- Can be the only cleanser for someone who wears little or no makeup.
- Rinses off, so it does not sit on the skin the way a balm can.

## Drawbacks

- A weak match for heavy, waterproof makeup or a thick layer of mineral sunscreen. Those need a first cleanse or a longer massage.
- Oily skin may still feel like a film is left behind. That shopper should use Fresh Gel Cleanser instead.
- The cream texture looks thick in the bottle. A little goes far. Using a large blob wastes it and is harder to rinse.

## Works best when

Skin is dry, tight after washing, or reactive to foaming cleansers. Also a good evening cleanser when the shopper wore Veil Skin Tint or Sheer Mineral SPF and does not need a full waterproof removal.

## Less effective when

The shopper wants a deep-clean foam, wears Lift Mascara in a heavy coat plus waterproof eye makeup from another brand, or has very oily skin in humid weather. It will not treat acne, eczema, or rosacea.

## How to use

1. Use a pea to nickel amount. Dry hands and a dry face, or a damp face, both work.
2. Massage for about 30 seconds, including the hairline if sunscreen was applied there.
3. Rinse with lukewarm water. Pat dry.
4. Follow with Calming Toner, then Dew Hydrating Serum and a moisturizer.

Morning: optional if the skin is very dry. Many dry-skin shoppers rinse with water in the morning and use this only at night.

Evening with makeup: massage longer. If mascara remains, go over the lashes with a damp cloth. This cleanser is not a dedicated waterproof remover.

## Pairs with

- Next: Calming Toner, Dew Hydrating Serum, Soft Daily Moisturizer or Rest Night Cream.
- Alternate cleanser: Fresh Gel Cleanser in the morning for combination skin, this cream at night.
- Do not expect it to replace Repair Lip Treatment. It is a face cleanser, not a lip balm.

## Shopper questions

### Is this the same as a makeup-removing balm?

No. It is a rinse-off cream. It handles light makeup and Sheer Mineral SPF. A balm is still better for heavy waterproof eye makeup. Lumen does not sell a balm.

### Can I use it with sensitive skin?

That is the intended shopper. It has no fragrance and no essential oils. Stop if the skin stings or rashes, and do not treat that as something the cleanser should fix.

### Will it foam?

Only a soft slip, not a rich lather. Lack of foam does not mean it is not cleaning.

### Can I use it around the eyes?

Yes, on closed lids, with a gentle massage. Keep it out of the eyes. It is not a tear-free shampoo and it is not oil-free.

### Does it clog pores?

It is a rinse-off product, not a leave-on cream. Shoppers who feel a film after rinsing are usually a better fit for Fresh Gel Cleanser.
