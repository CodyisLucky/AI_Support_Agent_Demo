# Calming Toner

## What it is

An alcohol-free watery toner used after cleansing and before serum. It is a comfort step, not an exfoliant and not an astringent. Shoppers who want a peel, a pore strip effect, or witch hazel are in the wrong product.

## Price and size

$26.00 for 150 ml. A palmful or two palm presses a day lasts about six to eight weeks.

## Ingredients

Water, glycerin, centella asiatica leaf water, panthenol, allantoin, and a mild preservative.

Not in this product: alcohol, witch hazel, fragrance, essential oils, glycolic acid, salicylic acid, or menthol. Nothing in it should tingle. A tingle means stop.

## Benefits

- Puts a little water and glycerin back after cleansing, which helps Dew Hydrating Serum spread.
- Centella water, panthenol, and allantoin are there for a calm feel, not a medical claim.
- Safe to use morning and night, including on days the shopper also uses Sheer Mineral SPF and makeup.
- Can be pressed on with hands, so a cotton pad is optional and wastes less.

## Drawbacks

- It will not shrink pores, close them, or exfoliate flakes.
- It is not hydrating enough to replace Dew Hydrating Serum or a moisturizer.
- Shoppers who want a "clean" tight feeling after toner will think this did nothing. That tight feeling is what this toner is designed to avoid.
- Cotton pads soak up a lot of product. Hands are the better method.

## Works best when

Used right after Cloud Cream Cleanser or Fresh Gel Cleanser, on skin that reddens easily from alcohol toners. Also useful as the damp layer under Dew Hydrating Serum.

## Less effective when

The shopper wants acid exfoliation, oil control, or acne treatment. Clear Clay Mask is the oil-control product, and only weekly. This toner is not a substitute.

## How to use

1. Cleanse and pat until the face is damp, not dripping.
2. Pour a pea to nickel amount into the palm, or mist is the wrong product. This is a pour.
3. Press over the face and neck. Do not scrub with a pad unless the shopper prefers it.
4. While the skin is still damp, apply Dew Hydrating Serum, then moisturizer.

Skip on broken skin or a rash. It is a cosmetic toner, not a wound wash.

## Pairs with

- Before: either cleanser.
- After: Dew Hydrating Serum, then Soft Daily Moisturizer or Rest Night Cream.
- Not a replacement for Dew Face Mist over makeup. The toner is a routine step on bare skin. The mist is the over-makeup spray.

## Shopper questions

### Does it have alcohol or witch hazel?

No alcohol and no witch hazel.

### Will it exfoliate?

No. There are no acids and no scrubby particles.

### Can I use it with sensitive skin?

That is the intended use. If it stings, rinse it off. Stinging is not a sign that it is working.

### Do I need a cotton pad?

No. Hands waste less and rub less.

### Can I skip serum if I use toner?

You can, but the toner is a thin step. Dry skin still wants Dew Hydrating Serum and a moisturizer.
