# Clear Clay Mask

## What it is

A weekly rinse-off mask made with kaolin and bentonite. It is for oily areas that look shiny or congested. It is not a daily cleanser, not a peel, and not a tool for pulling out blackheads.

## Price and size

$30.00 for 75 ml. Once a week, a thin layer on the T-zone uses little product. A jar lasts several months. Using it as a daily face mask will empty it faster and irritate the skin.

## Ingredients

Water, kaolin, bentonite, glycerin, allantoin, and a mild preservative.

Not in this product: charcoal, sulfur, benzoyl peroxide, salicylic acid, fragrance, essential oils, or scrub particles. It dries by water evaporating from clay, not by an active drug.

## Benefits

- Absorbs surface oil for a smoother look the same day.
- Glycerin and allantoin keep it from being as harsh as a plain drying clay.
- Can be used on the T-zone only, which is the better method for combination skin.
- Rinses clean. It does not peel off in a sheet.

## Drawbacks

- Daily use, or leaving it on until it cracks, leaves skin tight and flaky.
- It does not extract blackheads. Manual squeezing is not part of the instructions.
- Dry, sensitive, or already flaking skin should not use it.
- The matte look fades as soon as moisturizer and sunscreen go on. It is not oil control for the whole day.
- Bentonite can feel scratchy if the mask is rubbed while rinsing. Rinse, do not scrub.

## Works best when

Used once a week on oily skin or an oily T-zone, for about 8 to 10 minutes, then followed by toner and moisturizer. Best in the evening so the shopper is not rushing sunscreen on top of tight skin.

## Less effective when

Skin is dry, the barrier is already irritated, or the shopper expects acne to clear. Also a poor idea the same night as a long new experiment with a strong acid or retinoid from another brand.

## How to use

1. Cleanse with Fresh Gel Cleanser or Cloud Cream Cleanser. Pat until damp, not dripping.
2. Spread a thin layer on the T-zone, or the whole face only if the whole face is oily. Avoid the eyes and lips.
3. Leave it for 8 to 10 minutes. Rinse before it is fully cracked and ashy.
4. Rinse with lukewarm water. Pat dry.
5. Apply Calming Toner, Dew Hydrating Serum, and Soft Daily Moisturizer. Use Rest Night Cream only if the skin does not feel tight.
6. Repeat no more than once a week.

## Pairs with

- Cleanser before, hydrating steps after.
- Not a substitute for Fresh Gel Cleanser. The gel is daily. This mask is weekly.
- Skip Glow Face Oil the same night if the goal was less oil.

## Shopper questions

### Can I use this every day?

No. Once a week is the limit Lumen recommends. More often leads to tightness and flakes.

### Will it pull out blackheads?

No. Clay absorbs surface oil. It does not extract plugs. Do not tell the shopper to squeeze.

### Can I sleep in it?

No. It is a 8 to 10 minute mask. Sleeping in clay dries the skin out.

### My face is dry but my nose is shiny. Where do I put it?

On the nose and maybe the center of the forehead only. Leave the cheeks bare, then moisturize the whole face.

### Does it replace my cleanser?

No. Cleanse first. The mask is an extra weekly step.
