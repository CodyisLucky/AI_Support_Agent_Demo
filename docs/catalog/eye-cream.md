# Quiet Eye Cream

## What it is

A small-jar cream for the eye area, morning or night. It is a cosmetic moisturizer with caffeine for a temporary look of less puffiness. It does not erase wrinkles, bags, or dark circles that come from genetics, allergies, or sleep.

## Price and size

$38.00 for 15 ml. A rice-grain amount per eye, once a day, lasts about two to three months. Using a pea size per eye wastes it and makes concealer crease.

## Ingredients

Water, glycerin, squalane, shea butter, caffeine, niacinamide, palmitoyl tripeptide-1, and a mild preservative.

Not in this product: retinol, hydroquinone, fragrance, or essential oils. The peptide is a cosmetic ingredient. Do not promise collagen repair or wrinkle removal.

## Benefits

- A richer cushion than Soft Daily Moisturizer for dry under-eyes.
- Caffeine can make morning puffiness look slightly less for a few hours. The effect is temporary and mild.
- Small amount is designed to sit under Soft Concealer if it is tapped on and given a minute.
- Fragrance-free, which matters on the thinner skin around the eyes.

## Drawbacks

- Will not remove established wrinkles, hollows, or hereditary dark circles.
- Too much product creases and pills under Soft Concealer.
- Caffeine does not help puffiness caused by allergies, salt, or crying. Those have other causes.
- Shea and squalane can feel heavy if the shopper already uses Rest Night Cream up to the lash line.

## Works best when

The under-eye feels dry, or morning puffiness is mild. Use a rice-grain amount on the orbital bone. Let it sit before concealer.

## Less effective when

The shopper wants an instant eye lift, a filler effect, or coverage. Coverage is Soft Concealer. A very oily lid may not want this cream on the lid at all, only under the brow bone if the skin is dry.

## How to use

1. After serum and moisturizer, or instead of taking face cream right up to the lashes.
2. Take a rice-grain amount for both eyes. Dot under the brow and along the orbital bone, not on the waterline and not in the eye.
3. Tap with the ring finger until it disappears.
4. Morning: wait a minute, then Sheer Mineral SPF on the face, avoiding a heavy rub into the eye. Soft Concealer goes on after sunscreen, in a thin layer.
5. Night: this cream can be the last eye step. Rest Night Cream stays on the cheeks and does not need to be piled on the same spot.

## Pairs with

- Over it in the morning: a thin layer of Soft Concealer, then a light press of Soft Setting Powder if it creases.
- If concealer pills, use less eye cream or skip it on makeup days and use it only at night.

## Shopper questions

### Will this remove dark circles?

No. Blue or brown circles from vessels or pigment will still be there. Soft Concealer covers them. This cream only moisturizes and can slightly soften the look of morning puffiness.

### Can I use retinol with it?

This jar has no retinol. If the shopper uses a retinol from another brand, do not tell them to combine products. Suggest they ask the clinician who recommended the retinol.

### How much is too much?

If you can see a white film, or concealer rolls off, you used too much. A rice grain for both eyes is the amount.

### Can I use my face moisturizer instead?

Yes. Quiet Eye Cream is optional. Buy it when the under-eye is drier than the rest of the face or when morning puffiness is the complaint. Soft Daily Moisturizer and Rest Night Cream are fine around the eyes if they do not sting.

### Is the caffeine going to irritate me?

It should not tingle. If it does, stop. It is not a stimulant dose and it will not replace sleep.
