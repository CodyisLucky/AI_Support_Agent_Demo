# Glow Face Oil

## What it is

A leave-on face oil used in a few drops after moisturizer. It is the last skincare step at night for dry skin, or a mix-in when Soft Daily Moisturizer is not rich enough. It is not a sunscreen and not a cleanser.

## Price and size

$44.00 for 30 ml. Two to four drops a night lasts about two to three months. Pouring a palmful is the mistake that makes it feel greasy and run out fast.

## Ingredients

Squalane, jojoba oil, a small amount of rosehip oil, and tocopherol (vitamin E).

Not in this product: fragrance, essential oils, mineral oil, silicone, or SPF. Rosehip is a seed oil, not a fragrance. Shoppers who know they react to rosehip should patch test on the jaw for a few nights before using it on the whole face.

## Benefits

- Seals moisturizer on dry cheeks so skin feels soft in the morning.
- Squalane and jojoba are lighter than a heavy nut oil, so a small amount does not have to look wet.
- Mixes into Soft Daily Moisturizer when the shopper wants one step instead of two.
- Useful in cold, dry weather. Less useful in humid summer.

## Drawbacks

- On oily or congestion-prone skin it can add shine and closed comedones.
- Under Sheer Mineral SPF it often pills. Night is the right time. Morning use under sunscreen is not recommended.
- Rosehip can bother a minority of shoppers. The patch test matters.
- It will not replace Rest Night Cream. Oil seals. Cream moisturizes. Dry skin usually wants both, cream first.

## Works best when

Pressed on after Rest Night Cream or Soft Daily Moisturizer, at night, on dry or tight skin. Two to four drops for the whole face is enough. Also works mixed into a pea of moisturizer in the palm.

## Less effective when

The climate is humid, the skin is oily, or the shopper puts it on before sunscreen and makeup. Also a weak fix for flakes caused by over-washing or daily clay masks. Fix the routine first.

## How to use

1. Finish serum and moisturizer.
2. Warm 2 to 4 drops between fingers.
3. Press onto cheeks and anywhere that looks tight. Skip the T-zone if it gets shiny.
4. Stop when the skin looks soft, not wet.
5. In the morning, cleanse as usual so sunscreen does not pill over leftover oil.

Do not use it as a first cleanse. Lumen does not sell a cleansing oil. Cloud Cream Cleanser removes sunscreen.

## Pairs with

- After Rest Night Cream on dry skin.
- Not under Sheer Mineral SPF or Veil Skin Tint.
- Skip the same night as Clear Clay Mask if the point of the mask was less oil.

## Shopper questions

### Can I use this instead of moisturizer?

Only if the skin is already comfortable with moisturizer and you are adding oil for a bit more seal. Very dry skin should not drop the cream and use oil alone.

### Will it make me break out?

It can, if the skin is oily or already congested. Dry skin is the intended shopper. Stop if new closed comedones show up along the cheeks.

### Is it safe in the morning under makeup?

Lumen does not recommend it under Sheer Mineral SPF. The sunscreen pills. Use the oil at night.

### What does rosehip do here?

It is a small part of the blend for a softer feel. It is not a retinol and it will not fade scars. Patch test if rosehip has bothered the shopper before.

### How many drops?

Two to four for the whole face. If the skin looks glossy in a wet way, that was too many.
