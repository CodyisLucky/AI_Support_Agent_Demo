# Petal Cream Blush

## What it is

A buildable cream blush in one shade, Petal Rose, a warm rose. It is tapped on with fingers. It is not a powder blush. Lumen does not sell a powder blush.

## Price and size

$22.00 for 8 g. A pin-dot per cheek lasts several months.

## Ingredients

Squalane, waxes, caprylic/capric triglyceride, iron oxides and other pigments for Petal Rose, and vitamin E.

Not in this product: fragrance, glitter, or a cool baby-pink option. The shade is warm. On very cool, fair skin it can read coral. Say that before they buy if their coloring is cool.

## Benefits

- Melts into Veil Skin Tint instead of sitting on top like a powder.
- Buildable. One dot is a stain. A second dot is a clearer cheek.
- Fingers are the intended tool. No brush is required.
- A cream formula suits dry and normal skin better than a dry powder would.

## Drawbacks

- On very oily skin it fades or slides unless Soft Setting Powder is pressed on top.
- One shade. It will not give a berry, brown, or cool pink.
- Dragging it across the cheek makes streaks. Tapping avoids that.
- Too much product on dry patches grabs and looks patchy.

## Works best when

Tapped onto the apples of the cheeks over Veil Skin Tint, or onto bare moisturized skin for a no-makeup day. Dry to normal skin gets the most even result. Set with a light powder only if the cheeks get oily.

## Less effective when

The shopper wants a sharp contour, a cool pink, or all-day wear on very oily skin without powder. Also less even if the skin is flaking.

## How to use

1. Finish tint, or work on moisturized bare skin.
2. Warm a pin-dot on the pad of a finger.
3. Tap the apple of the cheek, then blend toward the temple. Stop before the hairline.
4. Repeat on the other cheek. Add a second dot only if the first looks too shy.
5. Optional: Warm Highlighter above it, then a light press of Soft Setting Powder if shine is a problem.
6. Stay Setting Spray can go on after the whole face is done.

## Pairs with

- Over Veil Skin Tint.
- Under a light Soft Setting Powder if oily.
- With Warm Highlighter on the cheekbone, not mixed into the blush or the rose turns muddy.
- Not a lip product. Satin Lip Color is the matching rose for lips, but they are separate formulas.

## Shopper questions

### What shade is it?

Petal Rose, a warm rose. There is only one shade.

### Can I use it on my lips?

It is a cheek product. Satin Lip Color is the lip color in a related rose. Do not promise the blush is safe as a lipstick. The formulas are different.

### How do I stop it from disappearing?

Oily skin should press Soft Setting Powder over it and finish with Stay Setting Spray. Dry skin usually does not need powder. Fading after a long day is normal for a cream. It is not a 12-hour stain.

### My cheeks look streaky.

The product was dragged. Tap a smaller amount. Do not rub back and forth.

### Can I apply it before foundation?

You can tap it on moisturizer and then sheer the tint over it, but the easier order is tint first, blush second. Under a heavy layer of tint it disappears.
