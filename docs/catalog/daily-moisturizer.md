# Soft Daily Moisturizer

## What it is

A daytime moisturizer with a soft, non-greasy finish. It is the default lotion under sunscreen and makeup. Rest Night Cream is richer and is the night option. This one is lighter.

## Price and size

$32.00 for 50 ml. A nickel-size amount once a day lasts about two months.

## Ingredients

Water, glycerin, squalane, caprylic/capric triglyceride, niacinamide at 2 percent, a low level of shea butter, dimethicone, and a mild preservative.

Not in this product: fragrance, essential oils, SPF, retinol, or acids. The 2 percent niacinamide is a comfort level, not a high-strength tone treatment. Do not promise fading of dark spots.

## Benefits

- Comfortable under Sheer Mineral SPF and Veil Skin Tint.
- Niacinamide at a low level suits shoppers who flush easily at higher strengths.
- Finish is soft rather than shiny, so it works for normal and combination skin.
- One jar covers face. Neck needs a second small amount.

## Drawbacks

- Not rich enough as the only cream for very dry skin at night. Use Rest Night Cream then.
- Dimethicone can pill if the shopper rubs sunscreen on top immediately, or mixes it with a silicone-heavy product from another brand.
- It is not a sunscreen. Daytime use still needs Sheer Mineral SPF afterward.
- Very oily skin in summer may prefer a half-nickel amount, or the serum alone plus sunscreen.

## Works best when

Used in the morning on normal, dry, or combination skin, after Dew Hydrating Serum and before Sheer Mineral SPF. Also fine at night for oily shoppers who find Rest Night Cream too heavy.

## Less effective when

The air is very cold and dry and this is the only night cream. Cheeks can still flake. It also will not mattify oily skin the way a powder does. Soft Setting Powder does that, and only after makeup.

## How to use

1. Apply serum if using one. Wait until it is no longer wet.
2. Warm a nickel size between fingers.
3. Press over the face. Add a bit more on cheeks if they are dry.
4. Wait about a minute, then apply Sheer Mineral SPF.
5. Makeup goes on after sunscreen, not under it.

## Pairs with

- Under: Dew Hydrating Serum.
- Over: Sheer Mineral SPF, then Veil Skin Tint.
- Night swap: Rest Night Cream.
- If makeup pills, use less serum and let this cream sit before sunscreen.

## Shopper questions

### Does this have SPF?

No. Sheer Mineral SPF is a separate product and the last skincare step in the morning.

### Can I use it at night?

Yes. Dry skin will usually want Rest Night Cream instead. Oily skin can stay with this one.

### Will the niacinamide irritate me?

2 percent is a low cosmetic level. Shoppers who already know they react to niacinamide should skip it. It is not a peel.

### Why does my sunscreen pill?

Most often too much serum, or sunscreen rubbed on while this cream is still wet. Use less serum, wait a minute, and pat sunscreen on.

### Is it fragrance-free?

Yes. No fragrance and no essential oils.
