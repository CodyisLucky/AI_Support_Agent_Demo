# Cream Eyeshadow

## What it is

A blendable cream eyeshadow in one shade, Soft Taupe, with low shimmer. It is a one-and-done lid color, applied with a finger. It is not a glitter and not a powder palette. Lumen does not sell other eye shades.

## Price and size

$20.00 for 5 g. A fingertip tap per eye lasts several months.

## Ingredients

Squalane, waxes, silica, iron oxides for Soft Taupe, a small amount of pearl for the low shimmer, and tocopherol.

Not in this product: chunky glitter, fragrance, or a matte black or a bright color. The shimmer is fine pearl, not sparkle. On camera it should not throw glitter dots.

## Benefits

- One finger tap covers the lid for a finished look without brushes.
- Soft Taupe is a neutral that works with Warm Rose lips and Petal Rose blush.
- Cream texture grips a dry lid better than a loose shimmer would.
- Low shimmer reads as healthy, not metallic, in daylight.

## Drawbacks

- It creases on oily lids unless a thin veil of Soft Setting Powder is pressed on top.
- One shade. It cannot build a cut crease or a colorful look.
- On very dry, crepey lids a thick layer looks heavy. Use less.
- The pearl can emphasize texture if it is packed onto crinkled lids. A sheer tap is better than a swipe from the pot.
- It can tug if the finger is dry. Warm it on the back of the hand first.

## Works best when

Used as a quick lid color on normal or dry lids, after skincare has sunk in and before Lift Mascara. A sheer tap on the mobile lid, blended up with a clean finger, is the look it was made for.

## Less effective when

The lids are very oily and no powder follows, or the shopper wants a bold or multi-color eye. Also less flattering packed onto very textured lids.

## How to use

1. Let eye cream, if any, sink in. Skip Quiet Eye Cream on the lid if the lid is oily. Use it only under the eye.
2. Warm a pin-dot on a finger.
3. Tap across the mobile lid. Blend the edge toward the crease with what is left on the finger. Do not add more product for the blend.
4. If the lid gets oily, press a veil of Soft Setting Powder over the shadow.
5. Then Lift Mascara.
6. Stay Setting Spray from a distance, eyes closed, after mascara.

## Pairs with

- Before Lift Mascara.
- Soft Setting Powder on oily lids only.
- Brow Pencil for the brow, separately. Do not use the taupe shadow as a brow filler. It is too shimmery.

## Shopper questions

### What color is Soft Taupe?

A soft gray-brown with low shimmer. Not black, not gold, not glitter.

### Will it crease?

On oily lids, yes, unless you press Soft Setting Powder over it. On drier lids it usually stays with a thin layer.

### Can I use a brush?

A finger is the intended tool. A dense brush works if it is clean and the product is warmed first. A fluffy powder brush will not pick it up.

### Is it safe for sensitive eyes?

It has no fragrance. Stop if the eyes sting or swell. Do not apply it on the waterline.

### Can I wear it alone, without the rest of the makeup?

Yes. That is a common way to use it: moisturizer, sunscreen, this shadow, mascara.
