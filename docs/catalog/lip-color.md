# Satin Lip Color

## What it is

A lipstick in one shade, Warm Rose, with a satin finish. It is comfortable and not matte, and it is not a high-shine gloss. Lip Liner in Warm Rose is the matching pencil. Repair Lip Treatment is the untinted balm underneath when lips are dry.

## Price and size

$18.00 for 3.5 g. A bullet this size lasts months with daily use.

## Ingredients

Ricinus communis (castor) seed oil, waxes, caprylic/capric triglyceride, squalane, iron oxides and pigment for Warm Rose, and tocopherol.

Not in this product: fragrance, flavor, menthol, glitter, or SPF. Castor oil is common in lipsticks. Shoppers who avoid castor oil should skip this bullet.

## Benefits

- Satin finish feels more comfortable on dry lips than a flat matte.
- Warm Rose lines up with Lip Liner and sits in the same family as Petal Cream Blush, so cheeks and lips can match without a second lip shade.
- Creamy enough to apply from the bullet. A lip brush is optional.
- Does not tingle or plump. Nothing in it is a stimulant.

## Drawbacks

- It transfers onto cups and food. It is not a transfer-proof or 8-hour meal-proof lipstick.
- One shade. Cool-toned shoppers who want a blue-pink will find Warm Rose too warm.
- On very lined lips it settles unless Lip Liner is used and dry skin is prepped with a pin-dot of Repair Lip Treatment.
- A thick layer of balm underneath makes it feather. Blot the balm first.
- No SPF. It is not sun protection for the lips.

## Works best when

Worn for everyday color, alone or with Lip Liner. Prep dry lips with a blotted pin-dot of Repair Lip Treatment. Outline and lightly fill with Lip Liner, then the bullet. Blot once for a softer stain that transfers less.

## Less effective when

The shopper wants a nude, a true red, a matte that survives dinner, or a gloss. Also less even on flaking lips. Treat the dryness for a few nights with Repair Lip Treatment before expecting a smooth line.

## How to use

1. If lips are dry: pin-dot of Repair Lip Treatment, wait, blot.
2. Lip Liner: trace the lip line and a light fill so the color does not feather.
3. Apply Satin Lip Color from the bullet, center outward.
4. Blot with a tissue. A second thin coat adds wear without a thick, slidey layer.
5. Expect to reapply after eating or drinking.

## Pairs with

- Lip Liner in Warm Rose. They are sold as a pair. The liner is $14.00.
- Repair Lip Treatment under it, blotted, not piled on.
- Petal Cream Blush on the cheeks if the shopper wants one rose story. The blush is not a lipstick.

## Shopper questions

### What shade is it, exactly?

Warm Rose. One shade. It is a warm rose, not a brown nude and not a cool pink.

### Will it stay through dinner?

No. It transfers. Blotting and Lip Liner help the edge. The shopper should plan to reapply. Do not call it long-wear.

### Can I wear it without the liner?

Yes. The liner is for feathering and for lips that are lined or uneven. A smooth lip can wear the bullet alone.

### Is there a matching liner?

Yes. Lip Liner, shade Warm Rose, $14.00.

### My lips are dry and the color looks chalky.

Use Repair Lip Treatment at night. In the day, a pin-dot, blotted, then color. A thick balm layer makes it worse, not better.

### Does it have SPF or flavor?

Neither.
