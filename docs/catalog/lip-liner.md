# Lip Liner

## What it is

A rose lip liner in Warm Rose, made to match Satin Lip Color. It defines the lip line and reduces feathering. It can be worn under the lipstick or, for a softer look, alone as a light fill. It is not a nude liner and not a long-wear stain.

## Price and size

$14.00 for a 0.8 g pencil. Sharpened and used as an outline, it lasts months.

## Ingredients

Hydrogenated vegetable oil, waxes, iron oxides for Warm Rose, and tocopherol.

Not in this product: fragrance, flavor, or a second shade. It is the same Warm Rose name as Satin Lip Color so the agent can say they match. They are not the same formula. The pencil is drier than the bullet.

## Benefits

- Gives Satin Lip Color an edge so color feathers less into fine lines.
- A light fill under the lipstick keeps the center from looking patchy on lined lips.
- Warm Rose matches the lipstick. The shopper does not have to guess a liner shade.
- Creamy enough to draw without tugging if the tip is sharp and the lip is not flaking.

## Drawbacks

- One shade. It is wrong for a nude, a red, or a cool pink lipstick.
- Used alone all day it feels drier than Satin Lip Color and can emphasize flakes.
- A blunt tip drags and looks uneven.
- It will not stop transfer of the lipstick onto a cup. It only helps the edge.
- Too much Repair Lip Treatment under it makes the line slide. Blot balm first.

## Works best when

Lips are prepped, balm is blotted, and the pencil is sharp. Trace just inside the natural lip line, then a light fill, then Satin Lip Color. Especially useful if color usually bleeds into lines around the mouth.

## Less effective when

The shopper wants a different lip color, a plump over-lined look far outside the lip, or a moist gloss. Overlining far outside the lip looks harsh with this rose and is not the instruction.

## How to use

1. Blot any Repair Lip Treatment so the lip is flexible, not wet.
2. Sharpen the pencil.
3. Rest an elbow on the table. Short strokes along the lip line, slightly inside the edge if feathering is the problem.
4. Lightly fill the lip so the lipstick has an even base.
5. Apply Satin Lip Color. Blot. Reapply color if needed.
6. Expect to retrace after a meal.

## Pairs with

- Satin Lip Color, shade Warm Rose, $18.00. They are a set in shade name.
- Repair Lip Treatment only as a blotted prep, not a thick layer.
- Not Petal Cream Blush. That is a cheek product in a related rose, not a liner.

## Shopper questions

### Does this match Satin Lip Color?

Yes. Both are Warm Rose. The liner is slightly drier, which is what keeps the edge.

### Can I wear the liner alone?

Yes, filled in lightly, for a softer stain. It will feel drier than the lipstick. It is not as comfortable as an all-day balm.

### Will it make my lips bigger?

No. Trace the natural line. Drawing far outside the lip is not recommended with this shade.

### My liner tugs.

Sharpen it, and do not use it on flaking lips. A few nights of Repair Lip Treatment first, then blot before the pencil.

### Is there another liner shade?

No. Only Warm Rose.
