# Repair Lip Treatment

## What it is

A thick, unflavored balm for dry lips. It can be worn alone or as a thin layer under Satin Lip Color. It is skincare, not a lipstick. It has no tint.

## Price and size

$14.00 for 12 ml. A pea size, a few nights a week, lasts a few months. It is the lowest-priced item in the skincare lineup.

## Ingredients

Petrolatum, lanolin, shea butter, and tocopherol (vitamin E).

Not in this product: flavor, fragrance, menthol, camphor, salicylic acid, or pigment. Lanolin is the ingredient to flag. Shoppers with a known lanolin allergy should not use it. Lumen does not offer a lanolin-free balm.

## Benefits

- Petrolatum and lanolin seal moisture in, so dry or cracked-feeling lips stay comfortable longer than a thin gloss.
- A thin layer under Satin Lip Color keeps the lipstick from catching on dry lines.
- No flavor and no tingle, so it does not make people lick their lips.
- Can be an overnight layer. A thicker night coat is appropriate. A thick day coat looks waxy.

## Drawbacks

- Lanolin allergy is a real skip. Ask if the shopper has reacted to wool alcohols or lanolin before.
- It is not a glossy lip product and it is not Satin Lip Color. Alone, it looks like balm.
- It does not treat cold sores, infection, or angular cheilitis. Those need a clinician.
- Over a thick coat, Satin Lip Color slides and feather. Use a pin-dot amount under color, not a heavy layer.
- It can wear off while eating. Reapply.

## Works best when

Lips feel dry, chapped from weather, or catch when Satin Lip Color is applied. Use a pin-dot under lipstick during the day and a pea size at night on bare lips.

## Less effective when

The shopper wants color, shine, or a plumping tingle. Color is Satin Lip Color. This balm has no tingle because menthol and camphor were left out on purpose. Also ineffective if the shopper is allergic to lanolin.

## How to use

1. Day under color: blot lips. Apply a pin-dot. Wait a minute. Blot again. Then Lip Liner and Satin Lip Color.
2. Day alone: a small swipe is enough. Reapply when lips feel dry.
3. Night: a pea size on clean lips. Leave it. It will transfer to a pillow.
4. Do not use it on broken, bleeding cracks as a medicine. A simple balm is fine on ordinary dryness. Open cracks that hurt should be seen by a doctor.

## Pairs with

- Under Satin Lip Color and Lip Liner when lips are dry.
- Not a substitute for those two. They are the color. This is the comfort layer.
- Skip it under lipstick if the lip line feathers. Too much balm causes that. Use Lip Liner on bare lips instead.

## Shopper questions

### Does this have a flavor or a tint?

No flavor, no fragrance, and no tint.

### I am allergic to lanolin. Can I use it?

No. Lanolin is a main ingredient. Do not suggest they try a little.

### Can I wear it under Satin Lip Color?

Yes, in a pin-dot amount, blotted, then liner and color. A thick layer makes the lipstick slide.

### Will it heal cracked lips overnight?

It softens ordinary dryness by sealing moisture. It is not a drug and it will not close a split overnight. Deep or infected cracks need a doctor.

### How is this different from Satin Lip Color?

This balm has no color and is mostly petrolatum and lanolin. Satin Lip Color is a warm rose lipstick with a satin finish. They are meant to be used together when lips are dry, not instead of each other.
