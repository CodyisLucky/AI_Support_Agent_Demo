# Sheer Mineral SPF

## What it is

A mineral face sunscreen, SPF 30, with a sheer tint. The only UV filter is zinc oxide. The tint is there to soften the white cast. It is not a foundation and it is not Veil Skin Tint.

## Price and size

$28.00 for 40 ml. A full face needs about 1/4 teaspoon, which is two finger-lengths. Used daily at that amount, a bottle lasts about three to four weeks. Using less means the SPF on the label is not what the shopper is getting.

## Ingredients

Non-nano zinc oxide at 18 percent, water, glycerin, caprylic/capric triglyceride, iron oxides for the sheer tint, and a mild preservative.

Not in this product: chemical filters such as avobenzone, octinoxate, or oxybenzone. No fragrance. No SPF boosters that would make it SPF 50. The label claim is SPF 30.

Water resistance is 40 minutes, not 80. It is not a sport sunscreen.

## Benefits

- Zinc-only filter for shoppers who avoid chemical filters.
- Sheer iron-oxide tint reduces white cast on light and light-medium skin.
- Sits on top of Soft Daily Moisturizer when each layer is given a minute.
- Face sunscreen. The tint is meant for the face, not the body.

## Drawbacks

- One sheer tint. On deep skin it can look ashy or pale. Lumen does not offer a deeper tint of this sunscreen.
- White cast is reduced, not gone, if the shopper uses too little and does not spread it.
- Can pill over wet moisturizer, too much Dew Hydrating Serum, or Glow Face Oil applied underneath.
- Not water-resistant enough for a long swim or a heavy workout. Reapply after 40 minutes in the water, and every two hours in the sun anyway.
- The tint is not coverage. Redness and spots still need Veil Skin Tint or Soft Concealer on top.

## Works best when

It is the last skincare step every morning, on a dry layer of Soft Daily Moisturizer, for light to light-medium skin. Also the right sunscreen to put on before Veil Skin Tint.

## Less effective when

The shopper uses a pea size for the whole face, skips reapplication, swims for an hour, or needs a shade match deeper than light-medium. It is also a poor body sunscreen because of the tint and the small bottle.

## How to use

1. Finish serum and Soft Daily Moisturizer. Wait until they are dry to the touch.
2. Measure two finger-lengths for the face. Include ears and the neck if those areas see sun.
3. Pat, then spread. Do not scrub, or it pills.
4. Wait a minute before Veil Skin Tint.
5. Reapply every two hours outside. After swimming or sweating past 40 minutes, reapply. Makeup on top makes reapplication harder. A light press of more sunscreen, or a hat, is the honest advice. Dew Face Mist does not add SPF.

## Pairs with

- Under: Soft Daily Moisturizer. Not Glow Face Oil.
- Over: Veil Skin Tint, then the rest of the makeup.
- Remover: Cloud Cream Cleanser at night. Massage longer than a plain wash. Fresh Gel Cleanser can be the second cleanse.

## Shopper questions

### Is this a chemical or mineral sunscreen?

Mineral only. Zinc oxide at 18 percent. No avobenzone, octinoxate, or oxybenzone.

### What is the SPF, and is it waterproof?

SPF 30. Water resistant for 40 minutes. It is not 80-minute water resistant and it is not sweat-proof for a long workout.

### Will it leave a white cast?

On light and light-medium skin the sheer tint keeps the cast low if you use enough and spread it. On deeper skin the single tint can look ashy. Lumen has no second shade.

### Can I mix it with foundation?

You can wear Veil Skin Tint over it. Do not mix the two in the palm. Mixing thins the sunscreen and the SPF is no longer the labeled value.

### Does the tint replace my skin tint?

No. The tint only offsets zinc. Veil Skin Tint is the coverage product, and it has no SPF.

### Why does it pill?

The layer under it was still wet, or Glow Face Oil was underneath, or too much Dew Hydrating Serum was used. Let moisturizer dry, and keep oil for the evening.
