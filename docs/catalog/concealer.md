# Soft Concealer

## What it is

A creamy concealer for spots and under the eyes. The only shade is Soft Light, the same shade family as Veil Skin Tint. It is not a color corrector. There is no peach, green, or yellow corrector in the shop.

## Price and size

$24.00 for 6 ml. A small dot per spot and a thin under-eye line lasts about two to three months. Painting a triangle under each eye uses it up and creases.

## Ingredients

Water, glycerin, squalane, waxes, silica, iron oxides for Soft Light, and a mild preservative.

Not in this product: salicylic acid, caffeine, fragrance, or a second shade. It covers. It does not treat blemishes.

## Benefits

- Medium coverage in a thin layer, higher than Veil Skin Tint, so it can hide a spot the tint cannot.
- Creamy enough for dry under-eyes if the amount stays small and Quiet Eye Cream has sunk in.
- Matches Veil Skin Tint Soft Light when both are on light to light-medium skin.
- Sets with a fingertip press of Soft Setting Powder where it creases, not a full-face bake.

## Drawbacks

- One shade. It will look ashy or mask-like on deeper skin, and orange or dark on very fair skin.
- It creases in under-eye lines if the layer is thick or if Quiet Eye Cream was heavy.
- It will not change the color of a blue under-eye or a red spot before covering it. There is no corrector. A thin layer still lets strong color ghost through.
- On dry flakes it grabs. On an open blemish it can sting. Do not put it on broken skin.
- It is not skincare. A covered spot is still a spot.

## Works best when

Dotted on a reddened spot or a thin line under the eye, after Veil Skin Tint and after eye cream has disappeared. Warm the product on the back of the hand, tap it on, and set only the part that creases.

## Less effective when

The shade Soft Light is wrong, the shopper wants color correction, or they apply a thick layer and skip powder. Also a poor cover for texture. Concealer does not fill a scar.

## How to use

1. Skincare, Sheer Mineral SPF, then Veil Skin Tint.
2. Warm a pin-dot on a finger.
3. Spots: tap the center and blend the edge. Do not wipe.
4. Under eyes: a thin line at the inner corner and the darkest shadow only. Blend. Skip the smile lines.
5. Wait a few seconds. Press Soft Setting Powder on those spots with a finger or a small puff. Do not sweep powder across the whole under-eye if the skin is dry.
6. If it creases later, press it out with a clean finger. Do not add another full layer.

## Pairs with

- Veil Skin Tint in Soft Light. Use tint first so you conceal only what still shows.
- Soft Setting Powder, pressed, not swept.
- Quiet Eye Cream only in a rice-grain amount, fully tapped in, or the concealer pills.

## Shopper questions

### Does it match the skin tint?

Yes, if both are Soft Light. Lumen does not sell other concealer shades.

### Can it cover a dark under-eye completely?

Not always. A blue or brown shadow can still read through a cosmetic concealer. Use a thin layer. A thick layer creases and looks worse. There is no peach corrector to sell with it.

### Will it treat a pimple?

No. It only covers. Do not put it on broken or weeping skin.

### Why does it look cakey?

Too much product, dry flakes, or powder swept on in circles. Use less, prep flakes with moisturizer, and press powder only where it creases.

### Can I use it without the skin tint?

Yes. On a spot, concealer alone is fine. For an even face, tint first and concealer second so the spot does not look like a different color.
