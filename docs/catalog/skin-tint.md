# Veil Skin Tint

## What it is

A light-coverage skin tint that evens tone and lets skin show through. Lumen sells one shade, Soft Light, for light to light-medium skin. It is not a full-coverage foundation and it has no SPF.

## Price and size

$36.00 for 30 ml. Four to six drops a day lasts about two months.

## Ingredients

Water, glycerin, caprylic/capric triglyceride, silica, niacinamide, iron oxides for the Soft Light shade, and a mild preservative.

Not in this product: SPF, fragrance, or a wide shade range. The iron oxides are the color. They are not a sunscreen.

## Benefits

- Evens mild redness and uneven tone without a mask look.
- Light enough that dry skin does not look cakey if moisturizer went on first.
- Fingers or a damp sponge both work. Fingers give a sheerer finish.
- Sits on top of Sheer Mineral SPF once the sunscreen is dry.

## Drawbacks

- One shade. Soft Light is wrong for deep skin and can be too dark for very fair skin. The shop cannot mix a custom shade. Do not invent other shade names.
- Light coverage will not hide dark spots, birthmarks, or active blemishes. Soft Concealer is the extra coverage, and it is the same shade family.
- No SPF. Sheer Mineral SPF still goes underneath.
- On very oily skin it can move unless a light press of Soft Setting Powder follows.
- Can pill if sunscreen or serum is still wet.

## Works best when

The shopper is light to light-medium, wants a "my skin but even" finish, and has already put on moisturizer and Sheer Mineral SPF. A damp sponge adds a bit more coverage. Fingers stay sheerer.

## Less effective when

The shopper needs full coverage, a shade outside Soft Light, or sun protection from the tint itself. Also less effective on dry, flaking skin. Fix flakes with the skincare routine first. Tint clings to flakes.

## How to use

1. Skincare, then Sheer Mineral SPF. Wait until the sunscreen is dry.
2. Dot 4 to 6 drops on the center of the face.
3. Spread outward with fingers or a damp sponge. Build only where redness shows.
4. Conceal spots with Soft Concealer. Do not try to cover them by piling on tint.
5. Optional: press Soft Setting Powder on the T-zone.
6. Cream blush and highlighter go on after the tint, while it is still slightly movable.

## Pairs with

- Under: Sheer Mineral SPF, not mixed together.
- Over: Soft Concealer, Petal Cream Blush, Warm Highlighter, Soft Setting Powder.
- Shade partner: Soft Concealer in Soft Light. They are meant to match each other. They will not match a deeper skin tone.

## Shopper questions

### How many shades are there?

One. The shade name is Soft Light, for light to light-medium skin. If that is too light or too dark in the shop mirror, do not sell it as a match.

### Does it have SPF?

No. Wear Sheer Mineral SPF underneath.

### Can I mix it with my sunscreen?

No. Mixing thins the sunscreen so the SPF 30 claim no longer applies. Layer tint over dry sunscreen.

### Is this full coverage?

No. It is light, buildable toward light-medium. Spots need Soft Concealer.

### Why does it look patchy?

Usually flakes, or product piled on a wet sunscreen. Smooth skincare, let SPF dry, and use less tint.
