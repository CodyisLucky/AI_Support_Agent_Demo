# Product consultation

## What it is

A 30-minute in-shop appointment to choose skincare and makeup from the Lumen catalog. A specialist looks at the shopper's skin and coloring, asks what they already use, and writes a short routine using Lumen products only. It is a product conversation, not a facial, not a medical visit, and not the professional makeup service.

## Price and what is included

$40.00, paid at checkout like any other cart item. Booking the service is the purchase.

Included:

- 30 minutes in the shop with a specialist.
- A look at skin type, dryness, oiliness, and whether Soft Light and Warm Rose are reasonable matches. Shade checks use the real Veil Skin Tint, Soft Concealer, Petal Cream Blush, and Satin Lip Color. No other shades exist.
- A written routine of Lumen product names, the order to apply them, and what to skip.
- Up to two product touches on the skin for shade or texture, such as a dot of tint on the jaw. Not a full-face makeup application.
- Answers about the products in the catalog files.

Not included:

- A facial, massage, extraction, peel, or lash service.
- Professional makeup. That is a separate service, id makeup-service, $85.00, about 60 minutes.
- Take-home full-size products. The shopper buys those separately. The consult fee is not store credit.
- Medical diagnosis, prescriptions, mole checks, or advice to stop a medicine.
- Products from brands Lumen does not sell. The specialist will not build a routine of outside products.
- A custom-mixed shade. If Soft Light is wrong, the specialist says so and does not pretend it matches.

## Benefits

- Prevents buying the rich night cream for oily skin, or the gel cleanser for skin that is already tight.
- Shade honesty: one tint, one concealer, one blush, one lip shade. The consult is where a shopper finds out before they buy.
- The written routine is a list the shopper can take to the cart, or ignore.
- Useful before a first Lumen order, or when a shopper owns three products and they are pilling.

## Drawbacks

- Thirty minutes is short. It is not a skin treatment and not a lesson in technique for a full face.
- It only recommends Lumen products. A shopper loyal to another brand may not get what they wanted.
- It cannot create a missing shade or a medical treatment.
- The $40.00 is for the time. It does not come off the price of products bought the same day.
- Arriving with a full face of makeup limits what the specialist can see. A clean face, or only the makeup they want help removing, is better.

## Works best when

The shopper wants help choosing a simple Lumen routine, is unsure between the two cleansers or the two creams, or wants to test Soft Light and Warm Rose in person. Best if they bring the products they use now, or photos of the labels, and arrive a few minutes early with a clean or lightly made-up face.

## Less effective when

They want a dermatologist, a facial, a full-glam trial, bridal planning, or a routine built from another brand. Send full-face makeup requests to the professional makeup service. Send rashes, infections, sudden hair loss, and changing moles to a doctor. Do not use the consult as a medical visit.

## How to use

1. Add Product consultation to the cart and check out. The purchase is the booking.
2. The appointment is in the shop. Checkout collects a shipping address because the cart always does. The service itself happens in the shop, not by mail. Tell the shopper the consult is in person. Do not invent a video option.
3. Arrive on time. Late arrival shortens the 30 minutes. The visit still ends at the scheduled end.
4. Say what is bothering them: tightness, shine, pilling, or shade. Mention allergies, especially lanolin, fragrance, and rosehip.
5. The specialist writes the routine. Buying the products is separate and optional.
6. If they also want a full face done, that is the professional makeup service on another booking, not an add-on squeezed into this half hour.

## Pairs with

- Any Lumen product the written routine names.
- Professional makeup is a different appointment. Do not describe the consult as including it.
- A shopper who only needs one fact, such as "does the sunscreen have zinc," can be answered from the catalog without booking this service.

## Shopper questions

### What do I get for $40?

Thirty minutes in the shop, shade and texture checks, and a written Lumen routine. Not products to take home, not a facial, and not a full makeup application.

### Is the fee credited toward products?

No.

### Can you diagnose my rash or acne?

No. The specialist can talk about which Lumen products are gentler or less heavy. They cannot diagnose or prescribe. A doctor should see a rash, an infection, or a changing mole.

### I already bought the wrong cleanser. Can the consult fix that?

The specialist can explain whether to use it differently or stop. The consult fee does not include a refund or an exchange. Do not promise those.

### Do I need this before I buy?

No. Shoppers who know they want Cloud Cream Cleanser can just buy it. The consult is for people who want help choosing.

### Can I do the consult and the makeup service the same day?

Only as two separate bookings. The consult is 30 minutes. Professional makeup is about 60 minutes and is a different service. Do not promise they can be combined into one discounted visit.

### What if Soft Light does not match me?

The specialist will say it does not match. Lumen has no other tint or concealer shade to sell in its place.
