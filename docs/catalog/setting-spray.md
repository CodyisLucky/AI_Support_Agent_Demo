# Stay Setting Spray

## What it is

A light makeup setting spray used after the face is finished. It helps tint, cream blush, and powder stay put longer. It is not waterproof, not a hairspray, and not Dew Face Mist. The mist refreshes. This spray holds.

## Price and size

$26.00 for 100 ml. An X and a T of spray, once a day, lasts about a month. Soaking the face uses it up and can make makeup look wet and then patchy.

## Ingredients

Water, a light acrylate polymer for hold, glycerin, aloe leaf juice, and a preservative.

Not in this product: alcohol, fragrance, SPF, or a waterproof film. The polymer is why it is not the same as Dew Face Mist. Do not tell a shopper it will survive a swim or a tearful event. It is a light hold.

## Benefits

- Extends the wear of Veil Skin Tint, Petal Cream Blush, and Soft Setting Powder compared with no spray.
- Glycerin and aloe keep it from being a dry, hairspray-like mist.
- Alcohol-free and fragrance-free.
- Can be used on a full Lumen face: tint, concealer, blush, highlight, powder, brows, eyes, lips. Lips still transfer. The spray does not make Satin Lip Color transfer-proof.

## Drawbacks

- Not waterproof and not sweat-proof for a workout.
- Too much spray, or spraying too close, leaves droplets that pit powder and mascara.
- It can feel tacky for a minute. That tack is the polymer. Fanning it dry is fine. Rubbing it is not.
- It does not replace blotting. Oily skin can still shine. Blot, do not pile on more spray over a wet shine.
- It has no SPF and does not refresh sunscreen.

## Works best when

Makeup is done, including powder if powder is being used, and the spray is held about 20 cm (8 inches) away. Two or three passes in an X and a T. Eyes and mouth closed. Let it dry before hats, scarves, or a phone against the cheek.

## Less effective when

The makeup is still wet from a heavy Dew Face Mist, the bottle is held a few centimeters from the face, or the shopper expects swim-proof or tear-proof wear. Also less useful on bare skin with no makeup. It is not a hydrating mist.

## How to use

1. Finish the face. Powder before spray if both are used. Spray before powder can make powder stick in droplets.
2. Shake.
3. Hold about 20 cm away. Close eyes and mouth.
4. Spray an X and a T, about two to four sprays total. Stop.
5. Let it dry. Fan it. Do not blot until it is dry.
6. Lift Mascara can speckle if the spray is close. Distance prevents that.
7. Satin Lip Color can be applied before or after. After is slightly fresher. Either way it still transfers onto cups.

Dew Face Mist is for later in the day if the face feels dry, and only as a far, light pass. Do not soak a set face.

## Pairs with

- Last step after Soft Setting Powder and the cream products.
- Not a substitute for Dew Face Mist, Calming Toner, or Sheer Mineral SPF.
- If makeup looks pitted, the bottle was too close or the powder was wet. Next time, dry layers and more distance.

## Shopper questions

### How is this different from Dew Face Mist?

This spray has a holding polymer. The mist is water, glycerin, panthenol, and beta-glucan and does not set makeup. Use this one to hold a finished face. Use the mist to refresh bare skin or a dry face, lightly.

### Is it waterproof?

No. It is a light hold. Swimming, heavy sweat, and crying can still move makeup.

### Why does my makeup look wet and patchy?

Too many sprays or the bottle was too close. Use two to four sprays from about 20 cm and let it dry without touching.

### Can I spray it before powder?

Lumen recommends powder first, then spray. Spraying first makes powder grab onto droplets.

### Does it have alcohol or fragrance?

No alcohol and no fragrance.

### Will my lipstick stay through dinner if I spray it?

No. Satin Lip Color still transfers. The spray helps the face more than the lips.
