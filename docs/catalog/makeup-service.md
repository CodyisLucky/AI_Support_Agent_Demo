# Professional makeup

## What it is

A full makeup application by a Lumen makeup artist, in the shop, for an event or an appointment. It uses Lumen makeup: Veil Skin Tint, Soft Concealer, Petal Cream Blush, Cream Eyeshadow, Brow Pencil, Lift Mascara, Warm Highlighter, Soft Setting Powder, Lip Liner, Satin Lip Color, and Stay Setting Spray, plus skincare prep from the catalog as needed. It is not a lesson series, not a bridal package, and not the 30-minute product consultation.

## Price and what is included

$85.00, paid at checkout. Booking the service is the purchase.

Included:

- About 60 minutes in the shop with a makeup artist.
- Skin prep with Lumen moisturizer and Sheer Mineral SPF if the face is bare and time allows.
- A full face: complexion, cheeks, eyes, brows, and lips, using the Lumen shades that exist. That means Soft Light tint and concealer, Petal Rose blush, Soft Taupe cream shadow, Soft Brown brows, black mascara, Warm Glow highlighter, and Warm Rose lips.
- Lashes separated with Lift Mascara. No strip lashes and no lash extensions.
- A finished look the shopper wears out of the shop. Stay Setting Spray is used. The look is still not waterproof and the lipstick still transfers.
- One reference photo if the shopper brings one, adapted to the shades Lumen actually sells.

Not included:

- Take-home products. What stays is what is on the face. Full-size products are a separate purchase.
- Strip lashes, individual lashes, lash extensions, or brow tinting.
- Hair, wardrobe, or a trial plus a second event day. One booking is one application.
- Airbrush, prosthetics, body paint, or special-effects makeup.
- A shade that Lumen does not sell. The artist will not color-match outside Soft Light. If Soft Light is wrong for the skin, the artist says so and can do eyes, brows, cheeks, and lips without forcing the tint.
- Medical cover for a procedure, a scar treatment, or camouflage of an injury. That needs a specialist the shop is not.
- The $40 product consultation. Technique questions during the hour are fine. A written skincare routine for a cabinet of products is the consult, not this service.
- Guests. The appointment is for the person who booked.

## Benefits

- A complete face by someone who uses these formulas every day, including how not to pill the sunscreen under the tint.
- Useful before an event when the shopper does not want to do it themselves.
- The artist can show a lighter or fuller version of the same products, within light-to-medium coverage. They cannot invent full coverage the tint does not have.
- The shopper sees whether Soft Light and Warm Rose work on them before buying the products.

## Drawbacks

- Sixty minutes is the appointment. Arriving late shortens the makeup. The end time does not move.
- One set of shades. A shopper who wants a different lip, a glitter eye, or a deep foundation will not get that here.
- No lashes and no waterproof mascara. An outdoor tearful event or a swim will not hold the way a waterproof, lashed look would.
- The fee is not a deposit on products and not store credit.
- Skin that is sunburned, broken out and weeping, or freshly peeled should not be made up. The artist can refuse the complexion portion. The fee is not automatically refunded. Do not promise a refund. A manager would have to decide that outside these docs.

## Works best when

The shopper has an event or a photo, brings one reference photo, arrives on time with a clean face or their usual skincare done, and is comfortable with Lumen's actual shades. Mention lanolin allergy, fragrance sensitivity, and contact-lens wear at the start. Lift Mascara is not waterproof, which matters for contacts and tears.

## Less effective when

They want a glam look the catalog cannot make, a shade outside Soft Light, false lashes, a bridal trial and a wedding day, or a medical cover-up. Also a poor booking if they are more than 15 minutes late. The artist will do less, not run past the hour.

## How to use

1. Add Professional makeup to the cart and check out. That purchase is the booking.
2. The service is in the shop. The shipping address at checkout is a cart requirement. The makeup is not mailed.
3. Arrive on time with a clean face if possible. If they wear their own moisturizer, they should still accept Sheer Mineral SPF if the artist needs a dry base. Heavy oils from home can make the tint pill.
4. Bring one reference photo, not a folder of unrelated looks.
5. Say if they wear contacts, have a lanolin allergy, or do not want mascara.
6. The artist works for about 60 minutes, sprays Stay Setting Spray, and the shopper leaves wearing the makeup.
7. Removal that night is Cloud Cream Cleanser, as in the mascara and sunscreen files. The artist is not on call for removal.
8. If they also want a written routine for what to buy, book Product consultation separately.

## Pairs with

- The makeup products listed above. Buying them afterward is optional.
- Product consultation is the separate 30-minute planning visit, id product-consult, $40.00.
- Do not describe this service as including a photoshoot, a kit, or a lesson card.

## Shopper questions

### What is included for $85?

About 60 minutes, a full Lumen face, sunscreen if there is time, and setting spray. Not take-home products, not lashes, not a second appointment.

### Can you match any skin tone?

No. Complexion products are Soft Light only, for light to light-medium skin. If that shade is wrong, the artist should not apply it. Eyes, brows, blush, and lips can still be done.

### Do you bring false lashes?

No. Lift Mascara only. It is not waterproof.

### Is this a good bridal booking?

Only as a single application, not as a trial plus wedding day and not for a party of bridesmaids. Each person needs their own $85 booking. There is no bridal package price.

### Can I get a lesson instead of you doing it?

This booking is an application. The artist can explain a step while they work, but a 30-minute product and routine conversation is the product consultation.

### What if I am late?

The appointment still ends on time. Less of the face gets done. Do not promise they can stay late.

### What if my skin is sunburned or I have a cold sore?

The artist will not make up broken skin, a cold sore, or a fresh burn. Do not promise a refund. Suggest they rebook when the skin is intact, and a doctor if it is a medical issue.

### Will the makeup last all night?

Stay Setting Spray gives a light hold. It is not waterproof. Satin Lip Color still needs reapplying after eating. The shopper does not take the lipstick home as part of the fee. If they want to reapply, they need to buy Satin Lip Color.

### Is the consult included?

No. Product consultation is $40.00 and is a different 30-minute booking.
