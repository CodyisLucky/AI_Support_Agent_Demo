# Fresh Gel Cleanser

## What it is

A clear gel cleanser that foams lightly. It is the cleanser for oily or combination skin. Cloud Cream Cleanser is the gentler cream for dry or sensitive skin.

## Price and size

$22.00 for 150 ml. A short pump, about a blueberry size, once a day lasts about three months.

## Ingredients

Water, glycerin, coco-glucoside, niacinamide at 1 percent, zinc PCA, and a mild preservative. pH about 5.5.

Not in this product: sulfates, alcohol, fragrance, essential oils, salicylic acid, or benzoyl peroxide. It is not an acne medicine. The niacinamide level is too low to claim a treatment effect.

## Benefits

- Lifts the oily feel Cloud Cream Cleanser can leave on combination skin.
- Light foam rinses clean without a high-pH squeak.
- Zinc PCA and a low niacinamide level are there for a fresh finish, not a medicated one.
- Works as a morning cleanser and as a second cleanse at night after cream cleanser removes makeup.

## Drawbacks

- On dry or barrier-stressed skin it can feel tight. Those shoppers should switch to Cloud Cream Cleanser.
- It does not break up heavy makeup or Sheer Mineral SPF as well as Cloud Cream Cleanser. Use the cream first at night if they wore sunscreen or tint.
- It will not clear breakouts. Shoppers asking for a treatment need a clinician, not a stronger wash.

## Works best when

Skin is oily or combination, especially in the morning, or as the second step of a double cleanse. Also a fit when Cloud Cream Cleanser feels like it never rinses off.

## Less effective when

Skin is dry, flaking, or stinging from other products. Also a weak first step for a full face of waterproof makeup.

## How to use

1. Wet the face.
2. Work a blueberry size into wet hands, then over the face for 20 to 30 seconds.
3. Rinse. The skin should feel clean, not stripped. If it feels tight, use less or switch to Cloud Cream Cleanser.
4. Continue with Calming Toner and the rest of the routine.

Evening double cleanse: Cloud Cream Cleanser first, rinse, then this gel.

## Pairs with

- After: Calming Toner, Dew Hydrating Serum, Soft Daily Moisturizer. Oily shoppers can skip Rest Night Cream.
- Do not follow it immediately with Clear Clay Mask every day. Gel plus daily clay is too much for most faces. Keep the mask weekly.

## Shopper questions

### Will this foam like a drugstore gel?

It foams lightly. It is not a big-bubble sulfate cleanser.

### Can I use it twice a day?

Oily skin often can. If the face feels tight by evening, use it only in the morning and use Cloud Cream Cleanser at night.

### Does it have salicylic acid?

No. Do not describe it as an acne wash.

### Which cleanser should I buy if I am combination?

Often both. Gel in the morning or on the T-zone, cream at night or on dry cheeks. If they will buy one, ask whether tightness or shine bothers them more. Tightness points to Cloud Cream Cleanser. Shine points to this gel.

### Is the niacinamide going to overlap with Soft Daily Moisturizer?

The cleanser is rinsed off and is only 1 percent. The moisturizer is 2 percent and stays on. Using both is expected, not an overdose.
