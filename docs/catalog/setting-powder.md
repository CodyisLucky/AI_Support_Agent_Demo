# Soft Setting Powder

## What it is

A finely milled translucent powder used to set Veil Skin Tint and Soft Concealer. It blurs a little. It is not a foundation, not a tinted powder, and not a baking powder meant to be left on in a thick layer.

## Price and size

$28.00 for 8 g. A light press on the T-zone and under the eyes lasts a few months. Sweeping a heavy layer over the whole face wastes it and looks dry.

## Ingredients

Silica, rice powder, and a small amount of boron nitride for slip. Iron oxides are not used as a tint. The powder is translucent.

Not in this product: fragrance, talc, color, or SPF. On very deep skin a thick layer of any translucent powder can still flash pale. Use a light press, not a coat.

## Benefits

- Keeps Soft Concealer from creasing as much, and keeps Veil Skin Tint from moving on the T-zone.
- Fine mill, so a light layer does not look chalky on light and medium skin.
- Can set Petal Cream Blush and Cream Eyeshadow when those slide.
- Pressed on with a puff or a finger is the method that looks soft. A dense swipe with a brush looks drier.

## Drawbacks

- On very dry skin, an all-over layer emphasizes flakes and lines.
- It is not coverage. It will not hide redness. That is the tint and the concealer.
- A thick "bake" looks cakey and is not the instruction.
- Translucent is not invisible in a heavy layer on deep skin. Keep the layer thin.
- It does not control oil for eight hours. It slows shine. Oily skin may still need a blot.

## Works best when

Pressed, not swept, onto places that crease or get shiny: under-eye concealer, the sides of the nose, the chin, and an oily T-zone. After tint, concealer, and cream blush.

## Less effective when

The whole face is dry. Those shoppers should set only the concealer, or skip powder. Also ineffective as a mattifier if it is dusted on and immediately covered with Glow Face Oil. Oil and this powder fight each other. Oil is a night skincare step.

## How to use

1. Let cream products sit for a few seconds.
2. Pick up a small amount on a puff, a clean finger, or a small brush. Knock the excess off.
3. Press onto concealer and any shiny area. Do not drag.
4. Stop when the cream no longer feels wet. The face should not look white.
5. Cream blush can go before this powder. If blush is applied after, the powder can make the blush patchy. Order is blush, then powder.
6. Stay Setting Spray is the last step, after powder, from about 20 cm away.

## Pairs with

- Over Veil Skin Tint, Soft Concealer, Petal Cream Blush, and Cream Eyeshadow.
- Not over Glow Face Oil.
- Not a replacement for Sheer Mineral SPF.

## Shopper questions

### Will this match my skin tone?

It is translucent, not a shade. A heavy layer can look pale on deep skin. Use a light press.

### Does it have talc or fragrance?

No talc and no fragrance.

### Can I bake with it?

Lumen does not recommend a thick bake. Press a thin layer and move on. Leaving a thick coat to "cook" looks dry with this powder.

### My under-eyes look crepey now.

Too much powder, or the under-eye was already dry. Use less, or set only the inner corner. Add Quiet Eye Cream at night, not more powder.

### Do I need it if my skin is dry?

Often no, except a tiny press on concealer if it creases. Skip the all-over step.
