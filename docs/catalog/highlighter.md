# Warm Highlighter

## What it is

A cream highlighter in one shade, Warm Glow, a champagne-gold sheen. It is tapped on with a finger along the cheekbones. It is not a glitter gel and not a full-face luminizer.

## Price and size

$22.00 for 6 g. A pin-dot per cheekbone lasts several months.

## Ingredients

Squalane, waxes, mica, a small amount of iron oxide so the sheen stays champagne rather than stark white, and tocopherol.

Not in this product: chunky glitter, fragrance, or a pink or bronze second shade. The mica is fine. Packed on in a thick stripe, it still shows texture.

## Benefits

- Melts into cream blush and skin tint instead of a powder stripe.
- Warm Glow flatters the same shoppers who wear Warm Rose lips, as long as the amount stays small.
- Finger application. No fan brush required.
- A little on the high point of the cheek is enough to look rested.

## Drawbacks

- It emphasizes texture, pores, and dry patches if the layer is heavy or placed on a flake.
- On oily skin the sheen becomes shine unless a veil of Soft Setting Powder softens it. Powder also dulls the highlight. Oily shoppers have to choose.
- One shade. On very deep skin a champagne cream can look ashy if it is swiped on thick. A tiny tap is the only way to test it. Do not promise it works on every skin tone.
- Not an eye-safe glitter. Keep it off the waterline. A tiny bit on the brow bone is optional and can emphasize dryness there.

## Works best when

Tapped on the cheekbone after Petal Cream Blush and before Soft Setting Powder, on skin that is moisturized and not flaking. The amount is a pin-dot, blended until you see sheen and not a stripe.

## Less effective when

The shopper wants glitter, a glassy full face, or a highlight on textured or flaking cheeks. Also fights with a heavy layer of setting powder on top.

## How to use

1. Finish tint and Petal Cream Blush.
2. Warm a pin-dot on the finger.
3. Tap the top of the cheekbone, the part that catches light. Blend the edges.
4. Optional tiny tap at the inner corner, only if that skin is smooth.
5. If it looks like a stripe, you used too much. Blend what is there. Do not add more.
6. Powder lightly around it if the face is oily, not a thick coat directly on the highlight.
7. Stay Setting Spray last.

## Pairs with

- After Petal Cream Blush, over Veil Skin Tint.
- Before a light Soft Setting Powder, knowing powder dulls sheen.
- Not mixed into the blush. Mixing makes a muddy rose.

## Shopper questions

### What shade is Warm Glow?

Champagne-gold cream. One shade. Not white glitter and not a deep bronze.

### Will it show my pores?

A thick layer will. A pin-dot, tapped and blended, shows less texture than a swipe from the pot.

### Can I use it as an eyeshadow?

It is a face highlighter. Cream Eyeshadow in Soft Taupe is the eye product. A tiny brow-bone tap is as far as Lumen suggests, and not on dry, crepey skin.

### Can I wear it without blush?

Yes. Cheekbone only is a complete way to use it.

### Why does it disappear when I powder?

Soft Setting Powder mattifies. If you powder directly over the highlight, the sheen goes away. Powder around it, or skip powder on that spot.
