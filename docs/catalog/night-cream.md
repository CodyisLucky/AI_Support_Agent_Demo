# Rest Night Cream

## What it is

A richer evening cream for skin that wants more than Soft Daily Moisturizer at night. It uses a ceramide-style barrier blend and a low level of bakuchiol. It is not a retinol and not a prescription.

## Price and size

$46.00 for 50 ml. This is the most expensive skincare cream in the shop. A pea to nickel size at night lasts about two months.

## Ingredients

Water, glycerin, squalane, shea butter, ceramide NP, cholesterol, fatty acids, bakuchiol at 0.5 percent, and a mild preservative.

Not in this product: retinol, retinoids, fragrance, essential oils, acids, or SPF. Bakuchiol is not the same molecule as retinol. Do not tell a shopper it replaces a prescription retinoid or that it will clear acne.

## Benefits

- Heavier cushion for dry skin overnight.
- Ceramide NP, cholesterol, and fatty acids are the barrier-style blend. They suit skin that feels rough by evening.
- Bakuchiol at 0.5 percent is a gentle cosmetic extra. Expect smoother feel over weeks, not a peel and not flaking from retinization.
- Fragrance-free, so it is easier to use on reactive skin than a perfumed night cream.

## Drawbacks

- Too heavy for oily skin in warm, humid weather. Those shoppers should stay with Soft Daily Moisturizer at night.
- Can feel greasy on the pillow if the shopper uses a large amount plus Glow Face Oil.
- Bakuchiol is not retinol. Shoppers seeking a retinoid result will be disappointed.
- Do not use it the same night as a long Clear Clay Mask session if the skin already feels tight. The mask is weekly. This cream is the nights you are not masking, or later the same night only if the skin is comfortable after rinsing.

## Works best when

Used at night on dry or mature-feeling skin, after Dew Hydrating Serum, as the last skincare step. Also a fit when Soft Daily Moisturizer is fine by day but cheeks flake by morning.

## Less effective when

Skin is oily, the climate is humid, or the shopper wants an acne or wrinkle drug. It is also the wrong morning cream because it has no SPF and the finish is too rich under Sheer Mineral SPF.

## How to use

1. Cleanse, tone, and apply Dew Hydrating Serum on damp skin.
2. Take a pea to nickel size. Press over the face. Avoid the lash line if also using Quiet Eye Cream.
3. Optional: 1 or 2 drops of Glow Face Oil pressed on top of dry cheeks only.
4. Leave it on overnight. Cleanse in the morning with the usual cleanser.
5. Morning cream is Soft Daily Moisturizer, then Sheer Mineral SPF.

Start every night if the skin is comfortable. If the shopper is also starting a strong product from another brand, they can use this cream on the off nights. Do not design a retinoid schedule for them.

## Pairs with

- Instead of Soft Daily Moisturizer at night, not usually both.
- Under a tiny amount of Glow Face Oil on dry patches.
- Not under Sheer Mineral SPF in the morning.

## Shopper questions

### Is bakuchiol the same as retinol?

No. It is a different ingredient at 0.5 percent. It should not cause a retinol peel. It also should not be described as a retinoid replacement.

### Can I use it in the morning?

You can, but you will likely look shiny and you still need Sheer Mineral SPF. The intended use is night. Morning moisturizer is Soft Daily Moisturizer.

### I have oily skin. Should I buy this?

Usually no. Use Soft Daily Moisturizer. Buy this only if the cheeks are actually dry and the T-zone is the oily part, and then use it on the cheeks.

### Will it clog pores?

Dry skin usually does well. Oily, congestion-prone skin may see more closed comedones if they use a thick layer. If that happens, stop and use the lighter moisturizer.

### Can I use it with the clay mask?

Not stacked in a thick layer on tight skin. Use the mask weekly, rinse, tone, and use a small amount of this cream only if the skin feels comfortable. Otherwise moisturize with Soft Daily Moisturizer that night.
