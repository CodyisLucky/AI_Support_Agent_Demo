# Lift Mascara

## What it is

A defining black mascara with a light lifting effect. It washes off with Lumen cleanser. It is not waterproof and it is not a volume or false-lash product.

## Price and size

$20.00 for 8 ml. Replace the tube about three months after opening, sooner if it smells off or the eyes itch. Do not thin an old tube with water or saliva.

## Ingredients

Water, beeswax, carnauba wax, iron oxides, acacia gum, and a preservative system suited to an eye product.

Not in this product: fibers for false volume, waterproof polymers that need a special remover, or a brown shade. The wash-off behavior comes from the gum and wax balance. Cleanser removes it. A cotton pad and Cloud Cream Cleanser speed that up.

## Benefits

- Separates lashes and adds a modest lift. Good for a daytime eye.
- Comes off with Cloud Cream Cleanser or Fresh Gel Cleanser. No separate waterproof remover.
- Black is the only shade, so it reads clearly on most lash colors without a brown-black option to confuse the shopper.
- The brush is a slim defining brush, not a fat volume brush.

## Drawbacks

- Not waterproof. Tears, rain, and a heavy workout will smudge it.
- Not dramatic volume and not a substitute for false lashes or extensions.
- Pumping the wand pushes air in and dries the tube out. Wipe the wand on the neck instead.
- Shoppers with very sensitive eyes may still react. Stop if the eyes itch, swell, or stay red. That is not a "breaking in" period.
- It will not hold a curl from a lash curler as well as a waterproof formula. Curl, then mascara, and expect some drop.

## Works best when

The shopper wants defined daytime lashes and easy removal at night with their regular cleanser. One or two coats. Let the first coat get tacky before the second.

## Less effective when

The shopper is swimming, crying, exercising hard, or wants a thick false-lash look. Also a poor choice over lash extensions. Oil and wax can loosen extension adhesive. Tell extension wearers to skip it or ask their lash technician.

## How to use

1. Curl only if the shopper wants to, before mascara, on clean lashes.
2. Wipe excess on the tube neck. Do not pump.
3. Wiggle at the base, then sweep up. One coat defines. A second coat adds a bit more.
4. If lashes clump, comb through while the coat is still wet with a clean spoolie. Lumen does not include a spoolie in this tube. The Brow Pencil spoolie must be washed before it touches wet mascara, or skipped.
5. Removal: massage Cloud Cream Cleanser over closed lids, rinse, then cleanse the face as usual. A damp cloth helps if a heavy coat was used. Fresh Gel Cleanser can be the second step. Do not scrub the eye.

## Pairs with

- After Cream Eyeshadow. Mascara last, before or after Stay Setting Spray. Spray can dot mascara if the bottle is too close. Close the eyes and spray from 20 cm.
- Remover: Cloud Cream Cleanser. Not Repair Lip Treatment and not Glow Face Oil.

## Shopper questions

### Is it waterproof?

No. It is made to come off with cleanser. Tears and sweat can smudge it.

### Will it give me false-lash volume?

No. It defines and lifts lightly. It does not contain fibers.

### How do I remove it?

Massage Cloud Cream Cleanser on closed eyes and rinse. A second pass with Fresh Gel Cleanser is fine. You do not need a separate waterproof remover because this is not waterproof.

### Can I wear it with lash extensions?

Lumen does not recommend it. Waxes can weaken extension glue. The shopper should ask the technician who applied the extensions.

### When do I throw it away?

About three months after opening, or sooner if it dries out, smells odd, or the eyes react.

### Why did it flake?

Usually too many coats, or a second coat before the first set. Use less and let the first coat get tacky.
