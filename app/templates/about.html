A local counter for skin and color

Lumen Beauty is a shop for skincare, makeup, and two in-shop appointments. Add what you want, review the cart, and check out. You can buy as a guest or sign in. Purchases stay on this machine.

Skincare

Cleansers, serums, moisturizers, and treatments for a daily routine. Each product on the shelf says what it is, when it helps, and when to leave it.

Makeup

Tint, blush, lips, eyes, and powder, in the shades the counter actually carries. A product that is out of stock stays on the shelf and cannot be added.

Appointments

A product consultation is 30 minutes in the shop to choose a routine from this catalog. Professional makeup is about an hour. Both are booked through the same cart as a cream. The consult is not a facial, and it is not the makeup service.
